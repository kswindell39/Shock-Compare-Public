# Shock Compare

Compare shock dyno sheets corner by corner on your phone. Add your own cars or drivers, scan a photo of a dyno sheet, paste text, or type the numbers in.

- **Private:** everything you enter stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Offline:** after the first visit it works with no signal, including photo scanning once the reader has downloaded.
- **Install:** open the link in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Photo reading uses [Tesseract.js](https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js) (Apache 2.0), included in `vendor/`.
